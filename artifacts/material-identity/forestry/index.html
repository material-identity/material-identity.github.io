ForestrySource and ForestryOutput

Purpose

Standardized documentation of harvested wood from the forest to the final product, supporting the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR), the US Lacey Act and similar legal harvest and traceability requirements.

Structure at a glance

Two schemas work together: ForestrySource (v1.1.0) records origin at harvest, and ForestryOutput (v1.0.0) records each supply chain transaction. ForestryOutput replaces the earlier Forestry schema (v0.0.1).

Key features

  • A single record instead of scattered documents.
  • Precise geolocation of harvest units.
  • Supporting documents secured by cryptographic hashes.
  • A verifiable chain of custody through references to earlier records.
  • Rendering in multiple languages.

Tools

  • Validation against the JSON schema, with valid and invalid test fixtures.
  • Rendering of any declaration as standardized PDF and HTML.
  • Localization in one or two languages, with localized numbers and dates.
